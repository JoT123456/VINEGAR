Home Overview

Learn more about the Flavor Predicting Platform, its purpose, and its main objectives.

Project Overview

The Flavor Predicting Platform is designed to help fermentation teams and quality analysts evaluate the sensory profile of a product batch. By combining data-driven predictions with intuitive visual reports, the platform helps stakeholders better understand flavor indicators, metabolite contributions, and sample deviations.

What the Tool Does

  • Predicts key flavor indicators such as acidity, freshness, and aftertaste.
  • Displays results with confidence intervals to support decision-making.
  • Highlights sample anomalies and batch deviations for quality control.
  • Provides interactive charts to visualize sensory and metabolite data.

Main Objectives

  1. Improve transparency in fermentation quality assessment.
  2. Support faster decisions with clear visual summaries.
  3. Help identify patterns and anomalies early in the production process.
  4. Allow teams to compare predicted sensory scores against reference values.
